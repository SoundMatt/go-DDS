go-dds-operator is installed.

1. Declare a participant:

   apiVersion: dds.soundmatt.io/v1alpha1
   kind: DDSParticipant
   metadata:
     name: sensor-pub
     namespace: {{ .Release.Namespace }}
   spec:
     domain: 3
     qosProfile: reliable-transient-local
     transport:
       kind: tcp

2. Annotate a pod (or its Deployment/StatefulSet template) to have it
   injected with DDS_DOMAIN_ID, DDS_QOS_PROFILE, DDS_TRANSPORT, and
   DDS_PEERS:

   metadata:
     annotations:
       dds.soundmatt.io/participant: sensor-pub

3. Optionally isolate a namespace's DDS traffic with a DDSDomain and bind
   the namespace to it:

   apiVersion: dds.soundmatt.io/v1alpha1
   kind: DDSDomain
   metadata:
     name: prod
     namespace: {{ .Release.Namespace }}
   spec:
     domainID: 3
     isolateNamespace: true

   kubectl label namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} dds.soundmatt.io/domain=prod

See https://github.com/SoundMatt/go-DDS/tree/main/k8s/operator for full
documentation.
